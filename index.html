About Me

Founder · GrantBase

Chukwuebuka Henry

I'm a first-year Software Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology Owerri (FUTO) and the founder of GrantBase — a verified opportunity discovery platform built specifically for Nigerian university students.

I built this resource hub because I believe access to quality study materials should never be a barrier. Every PDF here is free, watermarked with GrantBase's branding, and shared because a student somewhere needs it right now.

GrantBase is my way of solving a problem I live every day — the gap between Nigerian students and the opportunities that exist for them. One verified listing at a time.
